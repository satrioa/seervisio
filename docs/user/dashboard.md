# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a snapshot of your shop's performance at a glance.

---

## Business Health Card

**Purpose:** Shows the overall health score of your business.

The health score is a number from 0 to 100, calculated from multiple factors:
- Revenue trend
- Profit margin
- Inventory health
- SLA compliance
- Technician productivity
- Cash difference
- Customer return rate

**What to look for:**
- **Score > 80** — Business is doing well
- **Score 60–80** — Some areas need attention
- **Score < 60** — Significant issues need action

The trend arrow shows whether your health is improving (up), declining (down), or stable.

---

## Scoreboard

**Purpose:** Quick comparison of key metrics.

The scoreboard shows 6 metrics side by side:

| Metric | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Revenue** | Total revenue for the current period |
| **Margin** | Profit margin percentage |
| **Inventory** | Inventory health percentage |
| **Technicians** | Technician utilization percentage |
| **SLA** | On-time service completion rate |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | Average customer rating |

Each tile shows:
- Current value
- Trend (up/down/stable)
- Brief insight text (e.g., "Driven by Branch A")

> **Tip:** Hover over each tile for more details.

---

## Priority Alerts

**Purpose:** Highlight urgent issues that need your attention.

Alerts are color-coded by severity:

| Color | Severity | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Red | Critical | Immediate action required |
| Yellow | Warning | Needs attention soon |
| Blue | Info | For your awareness |

**Examples:**
- "LCD iPhone 13 — 2 pcs remaining. Expected to run out in 3 days."
- "Technician overloaded — 14 pending services vs team average of 7."

Click on an alert to take action (e.g., go to Inventory to restock).

---

## Today's Briefing

**Purpose:** A quick summary of today's highlights.

Shows:
- Revenue change compared to yesterday
- Number of overdue services
- Stock alerts (items running low)
- Margin notes
- Cash balance status

The briefing helps you start your day knowing what needs attention.

---

## Forecast Panel

**Purpose:** Predicts tomorrow's revenue and shows the 7-day trend.

- **Tomorrow's forecast** — Estimated revenue with confidence percentage
- **Next 7 days** — Bar chart showing daily projections
- **Recommendations** — Suggestions based on the forecast (e.g., "Prepare for weekend surge")

> **Note:** Forecasts are estimates based on historical data, not guarantees.

---

## Recommendations

**Purpose:** Actionable suggestions to improve your business.

Each recommendation shows:
- Title and description
- Expected impact (High / Medium / Low)
- Confidence score (0–100%)
- Estimated revenue protected

Click **View Detail** to see more information or take action.

---

## Insight Feed

**Purpose:** Chronological list of important business events and observations.

Insights are grouped by time:
- **Today** — Recent events
- **Yesterday** — Previous day
- **Last Week** — Older

Each insight includes:
- Category (Revenue, Inventory, Technician, Finance, Service, Customer)
- Severity (Critical, Warning, Info)
- Root cause analysis
- Supporting metrics
- Recommended action

Click on any insight to open the detail sheet with full information.

---

## AI Command Palette

**Purpose:** Ask questions about your business using natural language.

Press **Ctrl+K** or **Cmd+K** to open the command palette. Type questions like:
- "How was revenue this week?"
- "What items are low in stock?"
- "Show me technician performance"

> **Note:** The AI Command Center must be configured by your Master Admin before this feature is available.

---

## Filters

The dashboard data can be filtered by:

| Filter | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Branch** | Show data for a specific branch or all branches |
| **Period** | Today, This Week, This Month, This Year, Custom Range |
| **Date Range** | Custom start and end dates |

> **Tip:** Use filters to compare performance across branches or time periods.

---

## Statistics

Key statistics displayed on the dashboard:

- **Total Revenue** — Sum of all income
- **Total Expenses** — Sum of all expenses
- **Net Profit** — Revenue minus expenses
- **Services Completed** — Number of finished services
- **Average Ticket Size** — Average revenue per transaction
- **Active Services** — Currently in progress
- **Pending Services** — Waiting for parts or technician
- **Completed Today** — Services finished today

---

## Best Practices

- Check the Dashboard first thing every day
- Scan Priority Alerts for critical issues
- Review Today's Briefing to plan your day
- Use branch filters to compare locations
- Check the Forecast to prepare for busy days
