# Customers Module

The Customers module stores information about everyone who brings their devices to your shop.

---

## Adding a Customer

### During Service Creation

When creating a new service order:
1. Type the customer's name or phone number in the customer search field
2. If not found, click **+ Customer Baru**
3. Fill in the details:

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|:--------:|-------------|
| **Name** | ✅ | Customer's full name |
| **Phone** | ✅ | Active phone number for contact |
| **Email** | ❌ | Email address |
| **Address** | ❌ | Home or office address |
| **Notes** | ❌ | Any special notes about this customer |

4. Click **Simpan**

### From Customers Module

1. Go to **Customers** module
2. Click **Tambah Pelanggan** (Add Customer)
3. Fill in the details
4. Click **Simpan**

---

## Customer List

The customer list shows all your customers:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Name | Customer's full name |
| Phone | Phone number |
| Total Visits | How many times they've come to your shop |
| Last Visit | Date of their most recent service |
| Total Spent | Total amount they've paid across all visits |
| Status | Active or inactive |

### Sorting and Filtering

- **Search** — Find by name, phone, or email
- **Sort** — Click column headers to sort
- **Filter** — By date range, total spent, or visit count

---

## Customer Detail

Click a customer to see their full profile:

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Info** | Name, phone, email, address, notes |
| **Service History** | All past and current services for this customer |
| **Warranty** | Devices currently under warranty |
| **Devices** | List of devices this customer has brought in |
| **Total Spending** | Total money spent at your shop |

---

## Service History

Every service a customer has ever had is listed here:

- Service number
- Device info
- Status (completed, cancelled, etc.)
- Date
- Total cost
- Technician

> **Useful for:** Checking if a customer is a repeat visitor, identifying frequent issues, reviewing past repairs.

---

## Warranty Tracking

If a customer has devices under warranty:

1. Open the customer detail
2. Scroll to **Warranty** section
3. See which services are still covered
4. Expiration dates are clearly shown

> **Tip:** When a customer comes in with a warranty issue, look up their warranty status here first.

## Searching Customers

### By Name

Type the customer's name in the search bar. Results update as you type.

### By Phone Number

Search by phone number — this is often the fastest way to find a customer.

### By Device

Search by device IMEI or serial number if you know it.

> **Best Practice:** Always confirm the customer's name and phone when they arrive. Many people have similar names.

## Customer Notes

Add notes to customer profiles for important information:

- "Prefers WhatsApp communication"
- "Frequently complains about price"
- "Corporate account — send monthly invoice"
- "Always brings multiple devices"

To add a note:
1. Open the customer detail
2. Click **Edit**
3. Add or update the notes field
4. Click **Simpan**

---

## Best Practices

- **Capture phone number** — Always ask for the customer's phone number. It's the best way to search and contact them.
- **Add notes** — Record anything useful about the customer for future interactions.
- **Verify identity** — Confirm the customer's name and phone before handing over a device.
- **Don't create duplicates** — Always search first before adding a new customer.
- **Keep data clean** — Update customer info if they change their phone or address.

### Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Prevention |
|---------|------------|
| Creating duplicate customers | Always search before adding |
| Missing phone number | Make it required during service creation |
| Not linking to previous service | Search by phone to find existing record |
| Forgetting warranty status | Check customer warranty section first |
