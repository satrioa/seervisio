# AI Command Center

The AI Command Center gives you intelligent insights about your business — powered by your own operational data.

---

## What It Does

The AI Command Center analyzes your shop's data and presents it in an easy-to-understand dashboard. It helps you:

- **See your business health** at a glance
- **Spot problems** before they become critical
- **Get recommendations** for improving performance
- **Understand trends** in revenue, inventory, and service

> **Note:** The AI Command Center uses real data from your shop. It does not send your data to external AI services unless explicitly configured by your Master Admin.

---

## Dashboard Sections

### Business Health Score

A score from 0–100 that represents the overall health of your business.

**What affects the score:**
- Revenue trend (20% weight)
- Profit margin (20% weight)
- Inventory health (15% weight)
- SLA compliance (15% weight)
- Technician productivity (10% weight)
- Cash difference (10% weight)
- Customer return rate (10% weight)

| Score Range | Meaning |
|:-----------:|---------|
| 80–100 | Healthy — keep up the good work |
| 60–79 | Fair — some areas need attention |
| 40–59 | Concerning — take action soon |
| 0–39 | Critical — immediate action needed |

---

### Today's Briefing

A quick summary of today's key points:

- Revenue change compared to yesterday
- Number of overdue services
- Items that are running low in stock
- Margin changes
- Cash balance status

Read the briefing when you start your day to know what needs attention.

### Priority Alerts

Urgent issues highlighted by the system:

**Examples:**
- "LCD iPhone 13 — 2 pcs remaining. Expected to run out in 3 days."
- "Technician Rian has 14 pending services — redistribute workload."
- "Branch margin decreased 12% — review pricing."

Click any alert to go directly to the relevant module and take action.

> **Tip:** Use the forecast to plan staffing and inventory orders.

## How to Use

### Opening the AI Command Center

1. Go to **AI Command Center** from the sidebar
2. The dashboard loads with your current data
3. All sections update automatically

### Refreshing Data

Click **Refresh** at the top right to update all metrics with the latest data.

### Viewing Details

- Click an alert to take action
- Click an insight to open the detail sheet
- Click a recommendation to view more information

### Using the Command Palette (Keyboard Shortcut)

**Press Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+K** (Mac) to open the AI command palette.

Type natural language questions:
- "What's my revenue today?"
- "Show low stock items"
- "How's technician performance?"
- "Compare branches"

## Examples

### Scenario: Morning Check-in

1. Open AI Command Center
2. Read Today's Briefing: "Revenue up 5%, 2 overdue services, LCD stock low"
3. Check Priority Alerts: "LCD iPhone 13 critical — 2 remaining"
4. Click the alert to go to Inventory
5. Place a purchase order for LCD screens

### Scenario: Mid-day Review

1. Check Business Health Score: 85 (Healthy)
2. Scoreboard shows Margin trending down
3. Insights reveal: "Branch B margin dropped due to shipping costs"
4. Recommendation: "Review shipping strategy for Branch B"

### Scenario: End of Day

1. View Forecast for tomorrow
2. "Saturday expected to be 48% above average"
3. Recommendation: "Ensure extra staff coverage"
4. Plan staffing accordingly

## Best Practices

- **Check daily** — Make it part of your morning routine
- **Act on alerts** — Priority alerts are there for a reason
- **Review insights** — They help you understand trends
- **Use the forecast** — Plan ahead for busy periods
- **Compare branches** — If you have multiple locations
- **Refresh periodically** — Data updates throughout the day

### What the AI Does NOT Do

- It does not make decisions for you — it provides information
- It does not modify your data
- It does not automatically place orders or change prices
- It does not access data outside your Seervisio instance
