# Seervisio User Handbook

**Version 1.0**

---

## What is Seervisio?

Seervisio is an all-in-one repair shop management system. It helps you run every part of your repair business from one place — receiving customers, tracking repairs, selling products, managing inventory, handling cash, and viewing reports.

Think of it as your shop's command center.

---

## Who Should Use It

| Role | What They Do in Seervisio |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Shop Owner** | View reports, monitor branches, manage settings, track profit |
| **Branch Manager** | Oversee daily operations, manage staff, check performance |
| **Frontliner / Cashier** | Receive customers, create service orders, process payments, handle POS |
| **Technician** | Update service status, log spare parts used, complete repairs |

---

## Benefits

- **All-in-one** — No need for separate POS, inventory, and accounting tools
- **Real-time** — See what's happening in your shop the moment it happens
- **Multi-branch** — Manage multiple locations from a single login
- **Cash control** — Every rupiah is tracked from opening to closing shift
- **Inventory accuracy** — Know exactly what's in stock, what's low, and what's moving
- **Service tracking** — Know the status of every repair at a glance
- **Reports** — Understand your revenue, margin, technician performance, and more

---

## System Overview

Seervisio runs in your web browser. You can use it on a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or phone.

**You need:**
- An internet connection
- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
- A login account (provided by your Master Admin)

---

| Module | Purpose |

---

## How to Log In

1. Open your browser and go to the URL provided by your Master Admin
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click **Masuk** (Login)

If you don't have an account yet, ask your Master Admin to create one for you.

---

## Navigation

The sidebar on the left gives you access to all modules. Click any menu item to open it.

- The sidebar collapses on smaller screens
- Your current branch is shown at the top
- The Dynamic Island (top center) shows your shift status
